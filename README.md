# EG-System

موقع ويب React لشركة **المصرية الألمانية لتصنيع منتجات WPC & PVC**.

Egyptian-German Company for Manufacturing WPC & PVC Products — one of Egypt’s leading specialized manufacturers of WPC & PVC solutions for residential and commercial construction.

## Company overview

The company combines innovation and quality with the latest European technologies to produce WPC & PVC products featuring:

- Thermal and sound insulation
- Corrosion and environmental resistance
- Sustainable, modern aesthetics and long lifespan
